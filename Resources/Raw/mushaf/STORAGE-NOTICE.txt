Mushaf page storage: groups of up to ten original SVG files, encoded as MTHQPG01 (8 bytes), little-endian int32 first page and count, then little-endian int32 UTF-8 byte length and exact SVG bytes for each page. The complete container is Brotli-compressed. All original SVG bytes and all 604 pages are retained without modification. Upstream sources, licenses and original per-page metadata are retained alongside this notice.
